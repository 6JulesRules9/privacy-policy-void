## How to delete your data

### Option 1: Delete your leaderboard entry (in-app)

1. Open VOID
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**
3. Confirm the deletion

This removes your username and all associated scores from the global leaderboard immediately.

### Option 2: Reset all local data (in-app)

1. Open VOID
2. Go to **Settings → Reset All Data**
3. Confirm the reset

This deletes all locally stored game progress, settings, and statistics from your device.

### Option 3: Request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, send a deletion request to:

**void@julius-krassa.de**

Please include your in-game **Username#Tag** so we can identify your leaderboard entry.

We will process your request within **30 days** and confirm deletion by email.

---

## What data is deleted

| Data type | Deletion method | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| Leaderboard entry (Username#Tag, scores, playtime) | In-app or email request | Removed from server immediately |
| Local game progress (level, XP, settings) | In-app reset or uninstall | Stored only on your device |
| Advertising data (AdMob, Unity LevelPlay) | Via Android device settings → Privacy → Ads | Managed by Google / Unity |
